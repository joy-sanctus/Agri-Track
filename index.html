🌱 Soil pH Test Guide

Test BEFORE you buy fertilizer. If pH is wrong, fertilizer is wasted.

Home test:
Cup 1: Soil + Vinegar. Fizz = Alkaline.
Cup 2: Soil + Water + Baking Soda. Fizz = Acidic.
No fizz = Neutral.
